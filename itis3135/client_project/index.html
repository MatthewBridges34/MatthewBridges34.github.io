What Is Petal Press?

Petal Press is a flower-centered client project designed to help users identify flowers and understand how they can communicate emotion, culture, and meaning in journalism. Flowers often appear in ceremonies, memorials, protests, celebrations, portraits, and feature stories. This site helps explain what they are and what they may represent.

Why This Topic Matters

In journalism, details matter. Flowers can act as visual clues in a scene and can support deeper interpretation in photo essays, event coverage, feature writing, and cultural reporting. By understanding flower identity and symbolism, writers and viewers can better understand the story being told.
