Flower Care Tips

  • Place the bouquet in a clean vase with fresh water.
  • Trim the stems at an angle before placing them in water.
  • Keep flowers away from direct afternoon heat.
  • Change the water every two days.
  • Remove wilted petals or leaves to keep the arrangement fresh.
