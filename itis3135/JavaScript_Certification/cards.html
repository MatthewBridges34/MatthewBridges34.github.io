Featured Flower Cards

Carnation

Carnations are colorful flowers often used in bouquets and arrangements because they last well after being cut.

Care Tip: Keep in fresh water and trim stems every few days.

Daisy

Daisies are simple, bright flowers that represent freshness and cheerful garden beauty.

Care Tip: Place in moderate light and change water often.

Lily

Lilies are elegant flowers commonly used in formal arrangements and decorative displays.

Care Tip: Remove wilted petals and keep away from direct heat.

Rose

Roses are classic flowers known for their beauty, fragrance, and use in romantic or celebratory bouquets.

Care Tip: Trim stems at an angle before placing in water.

Sunflower

Sunflowers are tall, bright flowers that bring a warm and cheerful look to any garden or arrangement.

Care Tip: Use a tall vase and provide plenty of water.

Tulip

Tulips are spring flowers known for their smooth petals and clean, colorful appearance.

Care Tip: Keep in a cool area with indirect light.
