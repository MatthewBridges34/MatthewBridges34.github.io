Website Evaluations

F5

Design Analysis: Good

  • Contrast: The site uses visual contrast effectively by alternating background colors with font colors to create easy to read targets for consumers when navigating through the site.
  • Repetition: Each color, font, text size, heading, and background are all repeated consistently across the site, reinforcing brand identity and creating a cohesive experience for all users.
  • Alignment: The content uses a well thought out shape and pattern, utilizing rule of thirds which functions as a clear grid system for analytical eyes, to keep the eye set on the contents of the site.
  • Proximity: Each heading, paragraph, image, icon, hyperlink, and button are all meticulously placed in their spots logically and fluidly, this helps the users perspective.

Heaven's Gate

Design Analysis: Poor

  • Contrast: This website has poor color contrast with the background and texts, alternating from bright to dark, all in an effort to prioritize aesthetic over readability.
  • Repetition: No components of this website shows any signs of consistency in its texts, headings, and colors. Which creates the effect of the website looking archaic.
  • Alignment: Many elements and texts are misaligned and improperly centered, with strays laying about. The page conveys only clutter and gives off a non-chalant, insignificant appearance.
  • Proximity: No logical grouping of content, with various sections of information being placed on top of incorrect positions creating confusion for the user's interpretation of the website layout.
